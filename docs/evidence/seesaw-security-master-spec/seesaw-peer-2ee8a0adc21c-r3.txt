  ## Refuted or unverifiable claims

  ### R3-1 — MEDIUM: invented meaning of unresolved contradicts the contract

  Spec:203 says the master’s unresolved status is reserved for claims with no provider record. Neither source nor contract says this.

  The contract explicitly requires unresolved for material FIGI, issuer, or effective-time conflicts; candidate means incomplete but not materially
  contradictory. Spec:202 itself calls overlapping dates a material conflict but assigns candidate.

  Evidence:

  - docs/contracts/shepherd-security-identity.md:76–86
  - clients/security_master.py:203–204,217–223

  Both statuses are excluded from resolution, so immediate verified-membership safety is preserved. Their meanings are nevertheless contractual. Use unresolved
  for material contradictions and remove the invented reservation; missing identifiers alone may remain candidate.

  ### R3-2 — HIGH: new replay guard is not equivalent to PIT Silver

  Spec:140–147 replays all current non-rejected events and claims equivalence with PIT Silver. PIT Silver replays verified only, also applying its knowledge/
  effective-time cutoffs.

  Mixed candidate/verified histories can pass the proposed guard while producing an invalid verified history. The new whole-sequence check closes the earlier
  temporal bug but does not preserve the consumer’s status filtering.

  Evidence:

  - clients/pit_silver_revision.py:256–271
  - clients/index_membership_store.py:130–138
  - Spec:166–170 permits candidate or verified output according to the run’s confidence.

  Require the proposed change to preserve the verified-only consumer replay. Candidate-inclusive operator replay can be checked separately.

  ### R3-3 — LOW: repair consumer pins a different prefix

  Spec:275–278 groups shepherd_repair with membership-prefix consumers. Its _verify_identity reads SecurityMaster.events(), slices security_master_revision,
  verifies that prefix’s hash, and applies identity_as_of. It does not replay index membership.

  Evidence: clients/shepherd_repair.py:720–745. No membership references were found in that module.

  The membership-prefix statement is correct for livewire_scripts/shepherd_daily.py:55–59 and clients/pit_silver_revision.py:249–258. Correct the grouping
  without changing behavior.

  ## Counterexamples

  ### Mixed-status removal: guard passes, PIT Silver fails

  Hypothetical valid stored state:

  1. Security S has a verified identity.
  2. Index membership contains a candidate add for S at T1.
  3. An unresolved removal at T2 resolves to S and is processed with confidence B.

  The proposed non-rejected replay sees add → remove and accepts it. Store validation allows the verified removal because S has a verified identity. PIT Silver
  excludes the candidate add, then raises “membership removal has no open interval.”

  Evidence: clients/index_membership_store.py:135–138; clients/pit_silver_revision.py:262,268–271.

  The reverse mismatch is also possible: verified add → candidate remove → proposed verified add passes the combined replay, but verified-only replay sees two
  adds.

  Two provider observations have matching FIGIs but overlapping effective intervals. Spec:202 preserves them as candidates. The contract requires unresolved for
  this explicitly material effective-time conflict. This is a disposition mismatch, not a claim that these rows currently resolve.

  Evidence: docs/contracts/shepherd-security-identity.md:76–86.

  The previously reported crash, valid-removal, later-add, invalid-interval, coverage-skip, and timestamp-tie counterexamples are otherwise addressed by the
  revised text.
